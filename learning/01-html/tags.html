Markup

A markup language is a text-encoding system hich specifies the structure and formatting of a document and potentially the relationships among its parts.[1] Markup can control the display of a document or enrich its content to facilitate automated processing.


Elements

Hyper text markup language HTML imply a structure of nested elements.ese are indicated in the document by HTML tags, enclosed in angle brackets.[74][better source needed] In the simple, general case, the extent of an element is indicated by a pair of tags: a "start tag"

and "end tag"

. The text content of the element, if any, is placed between these tags. Tags may also enclose further tag markup between the start and end, including a mixture of tags and text. This indicates further (nested) elements, as children of the parent element.
